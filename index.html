Who we are

How it started

While Farah Bakes officially launched in 2022, the journey started much earlier when Farah, a passionate home baker, fell deeply in love with French artisanal baking techniques.

She swapped her career for baking sheets and set out on a mission to make the everyday staple a daily delight.

Sourdough Focus

We're obsessed by how levain and multi-day fermentation yield deeper flavors, more nutritious loaves.

A fresh take

We're re-inventing classics — always experimenting with new and seasonal flavors.

Artisanal Techniques

Our approach to breadmaking is rooted in French boulangerie techniques and savoir-faire.
